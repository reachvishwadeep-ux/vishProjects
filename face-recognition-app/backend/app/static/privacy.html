ReuniteNow Beta Privacy Policy

Effective and last updated: 24 November 2026

ReuniteNow is currently a limited beta for consented testing. It is not an emergency service, an official missing-person registry, or proof of identity, guardianship, custody, intent, or a safe handoff.

1. Information we collect

When you use the ReuniteNow beta, we may collect:

  • Account information: your phone number, one-time-code challenge records, login times, and security-session records.
  • Case content: whether a case is marked missing or found, its label, and the photos you choose to upload.
  • Face-analysis data: face-detection and image-quality measurements, numerical face embeddings, potential-match confidence scores, and the model version used. Face embeddings may be treated as biometric data under applicable law.
  • Connection activity: potential-match records, phone-sharing consent, connection status, and notification status.
  • Technical and security data: a random app-installation identifier, device platform, app version, build number, and security and audit events.

ReuniteNow does not require your email address and does not collect precise device location.

2. How we use information

We use this information to:

  • verify accounts and protect access to submissions;
  • validate photos and compare missing-person cases with found-person cases;
  • show potential matches and confidence information for human review;
  • let authenticated seekers and finders contact each other after a cross-account photo match;
  • detect abuse, investigate security events, and operate the beta; and
  • diagnose problems and evaluate the beta's reliability.

We do not use submitted photos or face embeddings to train a facial recognition model. We do not sell personal information or use it for advertising.

3. When information is shared

If the service identifies a potential match, authenticated participants in the matching cases may see the submitted case label, a case photo, confidence information, and the other participant's verified phone number. Phone numbers are shown only after both accounts have accepted the phone-sharing disclosure. A phone number cannot be taken back after another participant has seen or saved it. Similarity is evidence for review only and is not proof that two people are the same person.

We also use service providers to operate the beta:

  • DigitalOcean hosts the beta service in Bangalore, India.
  • Telnyx processes phone numbers and delivery metadata to send and verify SMS login codes.
  • Google Firebase processes an app-installation identifier and push-delivery metadata to send generic potential-match alerts. Push alerts do not contain photos, case details, scores, phone numbers, or private contact details.
  • Apple TestFlight distributes the iOS beta and may process installation, diagnostic, and feedback information under Apple's own privacy terms.

We may disclose information if required by law or when reasonably necessary to protect users, the service, or others.

4. Storage and retention

Uploaded case photos, face embeddings, case records, and related match data are stored on the beta service until they are deleted or the beta is reset. Security audit events are configured for a retention period of up to 365 days, and inactive app-installation records are configured to be deactivated after 365 days.

During the beta, request deletion of your account, cases, photos, and associated face embeddings by emailing the address below from a method that allows us to verify the request. Some limited security records may be retained when required for safety, fraud prevention, or legal compliance.

5. Security

We use HTTPS, access controls, short-lived access tokens, hashed refresh tokens, private database and object-storage networking, rate limits, and security audit records. No system can guarantee absolute security, especially during beta testing.

6. Consented beta testing

Beta testers should upload only their own photos or photos of another consenting adult. Do not upload real missing-person data, photos of children, or other sensitive personal data during this beta unless a future approved pilot supplies appropriate authority, consent, and safeguards.

7. Your choices

You may decline to upload photos, withdraw connection consent in the app, sign out, or ask us to access, correct, or delete your beta data. Removing the app from your device does not automatically delete data already submitted to the beta service.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the beta changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.
